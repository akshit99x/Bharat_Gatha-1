Later astronomers — Brahmagupta among them — attacked parts of my system hard, and some of their corrections were right. |

| `ARYABHATA-0006` | Konark & the Observatory · The Earth Does Not Stand Still | Add four to one hundred, multiply by eight, add sixty-two thousand: for a diameter of twenty thousand, the circumference is 62,832. | `ARYABHATA-0007` | Konark & the Observatory · The Earth Does Not Stand Still | As for notation: we write in words and in a place-value system where each position is ten times the last, and we need a mark for an empty place.
