# Devanampiya Piyadasi — Ashoka, emperor of Magadha

6 lines · about 3 minutes of speech.

Record each line on its own. Name the file with the ID in the first column (for example `DEVANAMPIYA-PIYADASI-0001.wav`).
Leave about half a second of quiet before and after each line. If you slip, record the whole line again.

| ID | Where it is heard | Say this |
|---|---|---|
| `DEVANAMPIYA-PIYADASI-0001` | Magadha · After Kalinga | I have taken Kalinga. The scribes give me a number: a hundred and fifty thousand deported, a hundred thousand killed, many times that number dead afterwards of hunger and sickness. I have never been given numbers after a victory before. I asked for them. Now my stonecutters wait, and I do not know what to tell them to write. |
| `DEVANAMPIYA-PIYADASI-0002` | Magadha · After Kalinga | Every king before me has done exactly that. Mine would be one more boast in one more language on one more rock, and the kings who read it would copy it. But I have begun to think that a king who can only be obeyed out of fear has to keep producing fear, and the supply is finite. |
| `DEVANAMPIYA-PIYADASI-0003` | Magadha · After Kalinga | Forgetting is a luxury of the people who were not there. And I find I cannot. The edict I am considering contains a sentence no royal inscription has contained before: that the emperor feels remorse. My advisors think it is weakness. I think it is the only durable thing I own. |
| `DEVANAMPIYA-PIYADASI-0004` | Magadha · After Kalinga | Then that is what the rock will say. And I will go further. The edicts will not be in the court’s Sanskrit but in the Prakrit people actually speak, in Brahmi where it is read and in Kharosthi in the north-west, and in Greek and Aramaic where my subjects read those. An order nobody can read is not an order. It is decoration. |
| `DEVANAMPIYA-PIYADASI-0005` | Magadha · After Kalinga | No. Read Rock Edict 12 when it is cut. It says that one should not praise one’s own sect and disparage another’s, that whoever does so from devotion damages the very thing they are devoted to. I pay for Buddhist, Brahmanical, Jain and Ajivika establishments alike. I am a Buddhist layman; my edicts are not a conversion order. |
| `DEVANAMPIYA-PIYADASI-0006` | Magadha · After Kalinga | Concretely: obedience to parents and elders, decent treatment of servants and the poor, generosity to ascetics of every sect, restraint in speech, and non-injury to living beings. I have planted shade trees and dug wells along the roads, set up rest-houses, appointed dhamma-mahamatras to inspect. I have given up the royal hunt. Judge me by the wells, not by the verbs. |
