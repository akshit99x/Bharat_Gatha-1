# Nabu-rimanni — Keeper of the sky diary

3 lines · about 1 minutes of speech.

Record each line on its own. Name the file with the ID in the first column (for example `NABU-RIMANNI-0001.wav`).
Leave about half a second of quiet before and after each line. If you slip, record the whole line again.

| ID | Where it is heard | Say this |
|---|---|---|
| `NABU-RIMANNI-0001` | Sumer & Dilmun · conversation | Every night, on a tablet: the moon’s place, the planets, the weather, the price of barley. |
| `NABU-RIMANNI-0002` | Sumer & Dilmun · conversation | Centuries of these. That is how we learned the eclipses come round in a cycle. |
| `NABU-RIMANNI-0003` | Sumer & Dilmun · conversation | Observe, record, wait. Most of astronomy is patience written down. |
