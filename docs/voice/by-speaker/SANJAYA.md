# Sanjaya — Narrator to the blind king

5 lines · about 1 minutes of speech.

Record each line on its own. Name the file with the ID in the first column (for example `SANJAYA-0001.wav`).
Leave about half a second of quiet before and after each line. If you slip, record the whole line again.

| ID | Where it is heard | Say this |
|---|---|---|
| `SANJAYA-0001` | Kurukshetra · after a challenge | I told the king what I saw and what I heard. I did not tell the king what to feel. That was the part the king could not forgive. |
| `SANJAYA-0002` | Kurukshetra · witness at the Sabha | I will tell you what I saw and what I heard. I will not tell you what to feel about it. That was never my job. |
| `SANJAYA-0003` | Kurukshetra · conversation | I describe this field to a king who cannot see it. He asks for details and then asks me to stop. |
| `SANJAYA-0004` | Kurukshetra · conversation | The whole poem reaches you through someone retelling it. Remember that. |
| `SANJAYA-0005` | Kurukshetra · conversation | Dhritarashtra’s first question is not who won. It is what my sons did. |
